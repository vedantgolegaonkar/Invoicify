Invoicify User Data Control

Request Account & Data Deletion

At Invoicify (developed by Herambh Software Technologies and Solutions), we respect your privacy and provide complete control over your personal, business, and client records.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the app installed, you can delete your data immediately without waiting for administrative review:

  1. Open the Invoicify app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings from the bottom navigation or menu.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account (below Logout).
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete Permanently.

All your records in Google Cloud Firestore (business profile, customers, inventory, invoices) and local session tokens will be permanently purged immediately.

Option 2: Submit a Web Deletion Request

If you uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit a deletion request directly by email:

  • Send an email to: herambh.info@gmail.com
  • Subject: Request Account and Data Deletion - Invoicify
  • Include: Your registered mobile number used in the app.
Click Here to Send Deletion Request Email

What Happens When Data is Deleted?

  • Account Details: Your user profile and authentication credentials are permanently deleted.
  • Business Records: Business name, address, GSTIN, bank details, logo, and Payment QR images are erased.
  • Customer & Invoices: All customer directory entries, ledger transactions, and saved invoice documents are permanently removed.
  • Timeline: In-app deletions take effect instantly. Email requests are verified and processed within 30 days.
