# Privacy Policy for Invoicify

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

**Invoicify** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and operated by **Herambh Software Technologies and Solutions**, is committed to safeguarding the privacy of business owners and their clients. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects personal and commercial data in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play User Data policies.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Invoicify collects and processes information solely to provide billing, ledger tracking, and invoice management functionality:

### A. Business Profile Information
When you set up your business profile, we may collect:
* Business name, trading name, and business address
* Contact telephone number and email address
* Statutory identification numbers (e.g., GSTIN, FSSAI license numbers, ARN number, MSME number)
* Bank account details (Account Name, Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC code, UPI ID)
* Business logo and Payment QR code image for customer payments

### B. Customer and Transaction Data
To facilitate invoicing and customer ledger management, you may input:
* Customer name, store/business name, contact numbers (phone and WhatsApp), and delivery address
* Product names, pricing, HSN/SAC codes, and inventory quantities
* Transaction details (invoice amounts, payment records, dates, and outstanding balance history)

### C. Device and Technical Diagnostics
We automatically collect minimal diagnostic data to ensure app reliability:
* Crash reports and application performance logs (via Google Firebase Crashlytics)
* Device model, operating system version, and unique installation identifiers

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:
* Create, manage, calculate, format, and generate GST-compliant PDF invoices and receipts
* Maintain accurate inventory, stock alerts, and customer ledger records
* Enable you to share invoices directly via WhatsApp, email, or other channels through your device's native share menu
* Provide automated calculations for sales, GST, and collection reports
* Identify and resolve crashes, bugs, and performance bottlenecks

**We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your or your customers' data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.**

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Cloud Storage

Your data is processed and stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure:
* **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Storage):** Used for cloud synchronization and secure data storage. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and encrypted at rest (AES-256).
* **Google Firebase Crashlytics:** Used for anonymous crash diagnosis and stability monitoring.
* **Google Fonts:** Used to render multilingual typography and currency symbols (₹) on PDF invoices.

---

## 4. Permissions Used by the Application

* **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to sync data securely with Firebase servers and generate reports.
* **Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Required to detect offline/online status for robust offline billing.
* **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to deliver low-stock alerts and payment reminders (if enabled).
* **Storage / Photos / Gallery:** Used solely when you choose to upload a Business Logo or Payment QR code image from your device gallery.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement strict administrative and technical safeguards to protect your records:
* Secure PIN lock protection to prevent unauthorized access on your device
* HTTPS/SSL encrypted data transmission
* Cloud Firestore security rules restricting access to authorized users

---

## 6. Data Retention and Account/Data Deletion

* **In-App Management:** You have full control over your data. You can edit, modify, or delete products, customers, and invoice records at any time directly within the app.
* **Complete Account & Cloud Data Deletion:** If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud records from our servers, you may request deletion by emailing us at **herambh.info@gmail.com**. Upon verification, all records will be deleted from our active databases within 30 days.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Invoicify is an enterprise billing and invoicing tool designed strictly for business owners and commercial use. It is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18.
