# Privacy Policy for Invoicify

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

**Invoicify** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and maintained by **Herambh Software Technologies and Solutions**, is committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your clients. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects personal and business information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Business Information
When you set up your business profile, we may collect:
* Business name, trading name, and business address
* Contact telephone number and email address
* Tax identification numbers (e.g., GSTIN, FSSAI license numbers)
* Bank account details (Account Name, Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC, UPI ID) for display on invoices
* Business logo and authorized signature image

### B. Customer and Transaction Data
To facilitate invoicing and ledger management, you may input:
* Customer name, store/business name, contact numbers (phone and WhatsApp), and delivery address
* Product names, pricing, HSN/SAC codes, and inventory quantities
* Transaction details (invoice amounts, payment records, dates, and outstanding balance history)

### C. Device and Technical Information
We automatically collect minimal diagnostic data to ensure app reliability:
* Crash reports and application performance logs (via Google Firebase Crashlytics)
* Device model, operating system version, and unique installation identifiers

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:
* Create, manage, calculate, and generate PDF invoices and receipts
* Maintain accurate inventory, stock alerts, and customer ledger records
* Enable you to share invoices directly via WhatsApp, email, or other channels through your device's native share menu
* Provide automated calculations for sales, GST, and collection reports
* Identify and fix crashes, bugs, and performance bottlenecks

We **do not** sell, rent, or monetize your or your customers' data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Data Storage

Your data is processed and stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure:
* **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Storage):** Used for cloud synchronization and secure data storage. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and encrypted at rest.
* **Google Firebase Crashlytics:** Used for anonymous crash diagnosis and stability monitoring.
* **Google Fonts:** Used to render multilingual typography and currency symbols (₹) on PDF invoices.

---

## 4. Permissions Used by the Application

* **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to sync data securely with Firebase servers and generate reports.
* **Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Required to detect offline/online status.
* **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to deliver low-stock alerts and payment reminders (if enabled).
* **Storage / Photos:** Used solely when you choose to upload a business logo or signature image from your device gallery.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement strict administrative and technical safeguards to protect your records:
* Secure PIN lock protection to prevent unauthorized access on your device
* HTTPS/SSL encrypted data transmission
* Firestore security rules restricting access to authorized users

---

## 6. Data Retention and Account/Data Deletion

* **In-App Management:** You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete products, customers, and invoice records at any time directly within the app.
* **Complete Account & Data Deletion:** If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud records, you may request deletion by emailing us at **support@herambh.com**. Upon verification, all records will be deleted from our active databases within 30 days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Invoicify is a business management and invoicing tool designed strictly for business owners and commercial use. It is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted in this document and accessible within the mobile application.
